Add the Direct Launch ("Instant Launch") module: `buildDirectLaunchTransaction` (the single LiquidityLauncher `multicall(createToken, distributeToken)` assembler), `encodeDirectLaunchConfig`, the Direct Launch preset constants (`DIRECT_LAUNCH_TOTAL_SUPPLY_RAW`, `DIRECT_LAUNCH_TOKEN_DECIMALS`, `DISABLED_CREATOR_FEE_BENEFICIARY`), `predictDirectLaunchTokenAddressCall`, and the chain-4663 `directLaunchStrategy` / `feeSplitter` / `beneficiaryVault` entries on the launcher address registry, carrying the 2026-07-27 contract deployment from the liquidity-launcher#196 fee-routing rework (fee-beneficiary registration lives on the new BeneficiaryVault; the launch calldata encoding is unchanged). Lifted into the SDK so server-side and client-side consumers build the same transaction from one implementation. Also adds the deterministic launch-pool derivation and quoting helpers — `getDirectLaunchPoolKey` / `getDirectLaunchPoolId` (the token's one hookless native-ETH v4 pool at the strategy's immutable `DIRECT_LAUNCH_POOL_LP_FEE`/`DIRECT_LAUNCH_POOL_TICK_SPACING`) plus `quoteDirectLaunchBuyCall` / `quoteDirectLaunchBuy` (`V4Quoter.quoteExactInputSingle` exact-in ETH→token, with the minimal `V4_QUOTER_ABI`) — so frontends and backends can quote fresh Direct Launch pools before routers index them.
