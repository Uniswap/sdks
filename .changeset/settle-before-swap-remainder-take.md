A route through a hook in `settleBeforeSwapHooks` now takes back, after the swap, the input currency of each hop through a listed hook, on the open delta. A listed hook may spend less than its hop's input and credit the rest to the router (for example a hook that fills from a limit-order book and returns what it could not fill); before, that credit was never taken and the swap reverted with `CurrencyNotSettled`. The unused input goes back to the caller when the caller paid the input (`payerIsUser`), so it never reaches a recipient that only expects the output, and to the recipient when a third party or the router funded the swap (`routerBalanceInput`, `ApproveProxy`). When the router wrapped or unwrapped the input for a listed first hop, the remainder is converted back and refunded in the currency paid (`UNWRAP_WETH` or `WRAP_ETH`). For a listed hook on a later hop, the refund is that hop's intermediate currency. A hook that spends the whole input leaves no credit and the take moves nothing. Mixed-route v4 sections through a listed hook get the same take. Routes through unlisted hooks are unchanged.
